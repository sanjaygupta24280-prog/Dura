Beyond

A performer’s profile built around craft, curiosity, and the belief that cinema can make us see one another more clearly.

“Theatre and cinema are spaces for emotion, healing, and transformation.”

I’m Sanjay Kumar Gupta, known professionally as DURA — an actor, filmmaker, creative director, and theatre artist from Nepal.

My path began in a small town in Madhesh and led me to Kathmandu, where theatre became a language for exploring human experience. I came to acting without a family background in cinema; I found my way through practice, collaboration, and a belief in stories that move people.

I’m drawn to emotionally layered characters and narratives that explore identity, belonging, dignity, and the quiet forces shaping our lives. I see performance as a meeting point between the personal and the universal — a place where audiences can recognize themselves in someone else.

My work in Elephants in the Fog brought me to the international film stage. I continue to develop as an actor and storyteller, carrying the voice and perspective of Nepal into new creative collaborations.

ELEPHANTS
IN THE FOGA FILM BY ABINASH BIKRAM SHAH
Nepali feature film · International premiere

Elephants
in the Fog

MJ · Actor

Directed by Abinash Bikram Shah. MJ is a rickshaw driver and Pirati’s neighbor, who transports the Kinnar family for work and festival visits. He carries a secret relationship with Apsara, Pirati’s daughter. When Apsara disappears during an elephant patrol, suspicion turns toward MJ.

The film was selected for Cannes’ Un Certain Regard and received the section’s Jury Prize — a milestone for Nepali cinema and a defining chapter in my journey as an actor.
